### 11.15 VARIABLE ALIGNMENT

`VARIABLE ALIGNMENT` sets the alignment of variables for display editing purposes. It does not affect the display of variables in the PSPP output. 
    
    
    VARIABLE ALIGNMENT
            var_list ( LEFT | RIGHT | CENTER )
            [ /var_list ( LEFT | RIGHT | CENTER ) ]
            .
            .
            .
            [ /var_list ( LEFT | RIGHT | CENTER ) ]
